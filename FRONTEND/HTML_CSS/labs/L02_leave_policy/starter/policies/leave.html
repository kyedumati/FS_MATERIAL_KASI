Leave policy This policy explains the types of leave at Acme and how to apply in AcmeHR. Numbers are sample values for this course. Leave types Casual leave — 12 days a year, for personal work. Sick leave — 12 days a year. Earned leave — 15 days a year. Unused days can be carried forward. Maternity and paternity leave — as per law and company policy. Loss of pay — when no balance is left. How to apply Open AcmeHR and choose Apply for leave. Choose the leave type and the dates. Add a short reason and submit. Your manager gets a notification and approves or rejects it. You get a notification, and your balance is updated. Changing a request You can edit or cancel a request while it is still pending. After approval, ask your manager.
