Leave policy

This policy explains the types of leave at Acme and how to apply in AcmeHR. Numbers are sample values for this course.

Jump to: Leave types · How to apply · Changing a request

Leave types

  • Casual leave — 12 days a year, for personal work.
  • Sick leave — 12 days a year.
  • Earned leave — 15 days a year. Unused days can be carried forward.
  • Maternity and paternity leave — as per law and company policy.
  • Loss of pay — when no balance is left.

How to apply

  1. Open AcmeHR and choose Apply for leave.
  2. Choose the leave type and the dates.
  3. Add a short reason and submit.
  4. Your manager gets a notification and approves or rejects it.
  5. You get a notification, and your balance is updated.

Changing a request

You can edit or cancel a request while it is still pending. After approval, ask your manager.
